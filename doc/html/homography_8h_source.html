25/* Return homography matrix determined from 5 points.
26 Applying M.perspective_transform on (0,0) will yield to ZERO.
27 Applying M.perspective_transform on (1000,0) will yield to X.
28 Applying M.perspective_transform on (0,1000) will yield to Y.
29 Applying M.perspective_transform on (1000,1000) will yield to XPY (X+Y).
30 Applying M.perspective_transform on (2000,3000) will yield to TXPY (2*X+3*Y).
31 If INVERT is true then inverse of this transformation is computed.
32 SCANNER_TYPE determines the scanner geometry. */
33trans_4d_matrix get_matrix_5points (bool invert, scanner_type type,
34 point_t zero, point_t x, point_t y,

analyze: weight overlap matching by collection support

Use retained per-channel collection support when matching analyzed screen
overlaps.  For every corresponding sample pair, combine the supports as

  w = s1 * s2 / (s1 + s2)

and use the same pair weight in the exposure estimate and in that channel's
squared RGB residual.  Normalize candidate scores by the accumulated red,
green and blue support.  This prevents inferred or poorly sampled screen
elements from overpowering reliable scanner data.

Keep geometric overlap qualification based on the historical count of known
screen tiles.  Preserve the existing suffix-sum and scoring path when support
arrays are absent, so fast collection retains its established behavior.
For packed Paget and Dufay channel lattices, average retained support over the
same physical subelements as red_avg(), green_avg() and blue_avg().

Add a weighted_matching regression covering the unweighted baseline, explicit
unit-support compatibility, an adversarial majority of low-support samples,
and independent per-channel weighting where only blue is reliable.  The
focused matcher, channel-sharpening, scanner-blur, denoising and lens-warp
groups pass in both O2 and Ofast checking builds.  The full six-stage Dufay
stitch fixture passes in both builds and retains its established -16,-10
offset.

The complete unit suite passes 41 of 42 groups in both builds.  The only
failure is the existing real-MTF fixture at SNR 29.8569 versus the threshold
30; the same failure was reproduced on the exact pre-patch 24de87bfd6b5 base,
and current main's intervening changes are confined to DNG lens normalization.
